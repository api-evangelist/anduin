# Anduin

> Anduin (Anduin Transactions) digitizes investor onboarding and fund operations for private markets — venture capital, private equity, and hedge funds. Its platform replaces paper-based subscription workflows with electronic fund subscription documents, secure data rooms, investor data management, e-signature, and an integration hub. As of this profile the company reports 1,305+ funds served, $244B+ capital raised, and 74,000+ investors subscribed. No public developer API or OpenAPI is published; this file indexes the public company, trust, and security surfaces.
