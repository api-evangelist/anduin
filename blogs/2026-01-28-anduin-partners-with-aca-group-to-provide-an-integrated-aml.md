New York, NY and London, UK — January 27, 2026 —ACA Group (ACA), the leading governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) advisor in financial services, and Anduin, a provider of investor onboarding and fund subscription technology, today announced a strategic partnership to deliver a fully integrated investor onboarding solution that combines advanced subscription technology with expert AML/KYC managed services and regulatory technology.
